# Help Wanted: Series Plan

## Premise
Vanilla survival Minecraft. The streamer is hired as a village's entire
Public Works Department by a villager she names "the Mayor." Every stream
starts at a job board where she reads the Mayor's work orders in his voice.
The village secretly owes years of back rent to its landlord, the Ender
Dragon. Week 3 reveals this. Week 4 is the eviction.

## Tone
Funny and chaotic, pointed at a goal. Workplace sitcom language for normal
Minecraft things: mining is overtime, dying is a workplace incident, chests
are filing cabinets. The Mayor is demanding, cheap, clueless, never grateful.
She is the overworked employee narrating to camera, mockumentary style.

## Cast
- The Mayor: name-tagged villager, the boss
- Gerald: the first mob that hit her in Session 1. Everything bad is Gerald's
  fault forever, even when Gerald is clearly dead.
- Officer Steve (or a chat-picked name): the iron golem security guard
- The Coworker: her first tamed pet
- The Landlord: the Ender Dragon, only seen in Week 4, referenced via letters
- Upper Management: the Warden. Feared. Never met by anyone who lived.

## Recurring bits (every stream)
1. Job Board: ring the bell, read numbered work orders. Format:
   WORK ORDER #014 / From: The Mayor / Priority: URGENT (everything is urgent)
   / Task: ... / Budget: 0 emeralds / Deadline: Yesterday.
   Numbers run continuously across the series.
2. Chaos Wheel: once per stream around the one-hour mark, chat spins a
   temporary "HR policy" modifier (pink leather armor only, no jumping,
   say "per my last email" before every attack, off-hand only, villager
   noises only, etc).
3. HR Incident Reports: any villager, pet, or golem death gets an official
   report read aloud (Employee / Cause / Witnesses / Outcome) and a poppy in
   the Memorial Garden.
4. Gerald: running blame counter on screen.
5. Performance Reviews: last stream of each week. Silly scorecard
   (Attendance, Teamwork, Budget, Overall), then a raise or write-up.
6. Trophy Wall: one item frame per week. Wk1 golden carrot, Wk2 blaze rod,
   Wk3 echo shard, Wk4 dragon egg.

## Pacing rules
No Nether before Session 5. No End before Session 14. No elytra until the
finale. No Mending trades until Session 11. If she finishes early, use side
quests instead of skipping ahead.

## Schedule
4 streams a week, 2 to 3 hours each, 4 weeks, 16 sessions. Short version
(8 sessions): 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 12, 15, 16.

## Sessions
Week 1: Onboarding
- S1 First Day on the Job. Orders #001-003: show up, build a break room,
  build the job board. Goals: survive night one, find the village, name the
  Mayor, full iron, meet Gerald. Ends on: "The village is a safety hazard.
  Also a zombie ate the baker."
- S2 Workplace Safety Violation. Orders #004-006: fix the hazard, hire
  security, put up safety posters. Goals: light village, wall, iron golem,
  banners, Memorial Garden, farm. Ends on: the Mayor says they're
  understaffed.
- S3 Hiring Season. Orders #007-009: more employees, a librarian, an office
  pet. Goals: villager breeding, tame the Coworker, assign jobs, storage
  "filing cabinets." Bit: job interviews for baby villagers, chat votes hire
  or reject. Ends on: quarterly deliverables are due.
- S4 Quarterly Deliverables. Orders #010-012: diamonds, a statue of the
  Mayor bigger than the church, attend review. Goals: diamond gear,
  enchanting table, ugly statue, Trophy Wall, Review #1. Ends on: "Pack a
  bag. Business trip."

Week 2: Expansion
- S5 Business Trip. Orders #013-015. First Nether trip, quartz, fortress,
  Nether hub. Bit: Expense Report of everything lost. Ends on: competitors
  spotted.
- S6 Hostile Takeover. Orders #016-018. Pillager raid on the village, totem
  of undying, Gerald blamed. Ends on: "We never had potions."
- S7 Supply Chain Issues. Orders #019-021. Blaze rods, brewing, piglin
  bartering as "vendor negotiations." Ends on: mandatory fun tomorrow.
- S8 The Office Party. Orders #022-024. Party hall, fireworks, chat-named
  villager guests, Review #2. Ends on: shareholders want a beach resort.

Week 3: Big Projects
- S9 The Beach Resort. Orders #025-027. Ocean monument raid, elder
  guardians, prismarine and sponges. Bit: formal complaint every time Mining
  Fatigue hits. Ends on: Upper Management wants a meeting, deep down.
- S10 Meeting Upper Management. Orders #028-030. Ancient city sneak, avoid
  the Warden, loot echo shards. Chat spams "shhh." Ends on: the village
  needs roads.
- S11 Infrastructure Bill. Orders #031-033. Nether highway, trading hall
  "cubicles," Mending unlocks, optional second village "merger." Ends on:
  a letter signed "The Landlord."
- S12 The Audit. Orders #034-036. Read the Landlord's letter (rent 4,000
  years overdue, "I have wings. I will find you."), the Mayor confesses,
  craft eyes of ender, Review #3. Ends on: "We're not paying. We're
  evicting him."

Week 4: Eviction
- S13 Hiring a Lawyer. Orders #037-039. Netherite, enchants, potions, a
  "legal briefing" gear montage. Ends on: time to serve the papers.
- S14 Serving the Papers. Orders #040-042. Find the stronghold, light the
  portal, a zombie named Gerald waits there. Ends on: "Wear something nice."
- S15 Eviction Day. Orders #043-045. Dragon fight, dragon egg is the
  "security deposit," final HR report: The Landlord, Evicted. Ends on: the
  landlord owned vacation homes.
- S16 Grand Reopening. Orders #046-048. End city, elytra, egg on the Trophy
  Wall, final review, the Mayor retires, she becomes Mayor, new sign on the
  job board: "Now hiring."

## Side quests (when a stream runs fast)
Customer Complaints (chat complaints to resolve), Lost and Found (find a
chat-named item in 15 minutes), Gerald's Revenge (build Gerald a prison,
he escapes), Employee Rehabilitation (cure a zombie villager), Company
Mascot (tame something weird), Casual Friday (chat designs her outfit),
Workplace Wellness (calm fishing or gardening), Team Photo.
